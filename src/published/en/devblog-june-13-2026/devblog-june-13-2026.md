Our latest platform updates are live as of **June 13, 2026** at **<span class="text-white">Voxi</span><span class="text-amber-300">Host</span>**. We are committed to making our cloud hosting services more accessible, global, and user-friendly. In this release, we have focused on a major overhaul of our currency and billing systems, along with handy dashboard refinements to keep you updated on all new platform features.

From a complete rebranding of our platform currency to native multi-currency payment support, here is everything we’ve rolled out over the past few weeks.

## 1. Transition to Credits & Multi-Currency Support

To better serve our growing international user base, we are transitioning from vPLN to **Credits**. This change lays the foundation for our global expansion, ensuring a consistent billing experience regardless of where you are hosting your servers.

Along with this rebranding, we are proud to launch native **multi-currency support**. You can now select your preferred billing currency from **PLN, USD, or EUR**. By paying in your local currency, you can completely bypass bank conversion fees and enjoy a more transparent billing experience.
